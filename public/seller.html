Seller Terms & Conditions

Product authenticity. Sellers must list genuine, lawful products, provide accurate descriptions and images, disclose defects, and maintain evidence of sourcing. Counterfeit, prohibited, unsafe, or misleading listings may be removed.

Orders, delivery and COD. Sellers must keep stock accurate, accept or reject orders promptly, pack orders safely, dispatch within the stated handling time, provide valid tracking, and cooperate with delivery and COD confirmation. Sellers must not request off-platform payment or misrepresent delivery status.

Platform commission and settlement. OmniMart may charge the commission and service fees shown to the seller before a sale or in the seller dashboard. Applicable fees may be deducted from payouts. COD proceeds are settled after delivery reconciliation, returns, chargebacks, and other disclosed adjustments.

Returns, refunds and disputes. Sellers must follow applicable law and OmniMart’s published return/refund process, respond to disputes with evidence, and accept responsibility for counterfeit, damaged, materially misdescribed, or incorrectly fulfilled orders. Refunds and return shipping are handled under the applicable policy.

Account security and data. Keep passwords and OTPs private, use accurate contact information, promptly report suspected compromise, and do not access another person’s account. OmniMart may suspend access to investigate fraud or policy violations. Seller information is used to operate the marketplace and meet legal obligations.

Updates and termination. Continued use after reasonable notice of updated terms means acceptance. Sellers may stop using the service; pending orders, returns, fees, and legal obligations remain due.
