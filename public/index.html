    1. Order Acceptance & Verification

    All orders placed on OmniMart PK are subject to phone or WhatsApp verification before dispatching Cash-on-Delivery (COD) orders across Pakistan.

    All product prices are listed in PKR. Stock availability and promotional prices are subject to change without prior notice.

    3. Cash on Delivery (COD) Rules

    Customers must pay the full invoice amount in cash to the delivery courier rider upon arrival. As per standard courier SOPs in Pakistan, packages cannot be opened prior to payment.

    4. Order Cancellation

    Orders can be cancelled within 12 hours of placement or before dispatch by contacting customer support (+92 314 4039790 / sajid.sk443@gmail.com). Repeated fake orders or unaccepted COD deliveries will result in permanent address blacklisting.

    1. 7-Day Inspection Warranty

    Customers can claim a replacement or refund within 7 days of delivery ONLY if the product received is broken, physically defective, or wrong item shipped.

    DAMAGE & MISUSE EXCLUSION (اہلیت کا استثنیٰ): Products damaged due to customer misuse, accidental drops, water exposure, burnt circuits, or unauthorized repair ARE STRICTLY NON-RETURNABLE AND NON-REFUNDABLE.

    2. Eligibility Conditions

    The item must be unused, in its original packaging, with all accessories, seals, manuals, and tags intact.

    3. Refund Method

    Approved refunds for COD orders will be processed via Easypaisa, JazzCash, or Direct Bank Transfer within 3-5 business days following physical inspection at our fulfillment warehouse.
